# AOI defect handling

Sample lab note for retrieval practice. Not a factory instruction, and not an extract from a standard.

## Open

1. Locate the sample open flag on the net note.
2. Check that the lead is sitting off the sample pad.
3. Move the board to the sample touch-up tray.

## Short

1. Find the sample short between the two named nets.
2. Look for a solder bridge on the sample photo note.
3. Stop the board and call the sample rework step.

## Mousebite

1. Match the sample mousebite mark to the board edge.
2. Leave the edge alone in this sample flow.
3. Record the sample board id and set the board aside.

## Spur

1. Find the sample spur on the trace.
2. Confirm it is extra copper and not a stain on the sample photo.
3. Route the board to the sample review bin.

## Copper

Copper here means spurious copper. This sample uses that name the same way the AOI class list does.

1. Mark the sample copper flag on the lot sheet.
2. Compare the pad with the sample reference note.
3. Leave the copper in place for the sample review.

## Pin-hole

1. Find the sample pin-hole in the pad fill.
2. Note the sample pad id on the practice sheet.
3. Send the board on to the sample rework SOP.
