# Reflow soldering SOP

Sample lab SOP for retrieval practice. Not a factory profile, and the zones below have no real degrees or seconds.

## Purpose

This sample walks a board through a pretend reflow oven. Read it as practice text.

## Paste

1. Align the sample stencil to the board fiducials.
2. Make one sample print and look at the pads.
3. Wipe the stencil when the sample print smears.

## Profile

The sample profile has a preheat zone, a soak zone, and a peak zone. This fixture does not list a temperature or a time. Leave the oven controls alone.

## Cooling

Let the sample board exit onto the sample rack. Wait until the practice note says the board is safe to hold.
